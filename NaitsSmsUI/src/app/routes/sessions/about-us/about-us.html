About Us

NAITS-ASMS

A smarter and simpler way to manage your school

NAITS-ASMS is a comprehensive, cloud-based School Management System from New Age Solutions Limited (NASL), designed to help schools manage their daily operations more easily, efficiently, and professionally.

From Pre-Nursery and Nursery to Primary and Secondary, NAITS-ASMS brings the essential functions of your school together in one platform. Manage student results, attendance, school fees, payroll, expenses, student records, and other school activities without having to rely on multiple systems or piles of paperwork.

With NAITS-ASMS, school proprietors and administrators can see what is happening across the school and make better-informed decisions. Teachers and administrative staff can spend less time on manual paperwork and more time focusing on teaching, students, and the smooth running of the school.

Save time, improve accuracy, strengthen financial control, and manage your school with greater confidence—all from one easy-to-use platform.

Schools have trusted NAITS-ASMS since 2017. The platform has been developed and refined around the practical needs of real schools, making it suitable for schools of different sizes and operating environments.

Our Story

Built from real school experience

New Age IT Solutions (NAITS) was established in 2015 by Engr. Bashir Sani. The idea for NAITS-ASMS started in 2014 while he was working as a teacher and experiencing first-hand the challenges schools faced with result processing, student records, attendance, and financial management.

The goal was simple: build a school management system that is affordable, easy to use, accessible on phones and computers, and practical for Nigerian schools. That vision has continued to guide the development of NAITS-ASMS as schools and their needs have grown.

For Your Entire School

Everyone gets the tools they need

NAITS-ASMS gives each member of your school team access to the functions relevant to their role. School administrators get a clear view of school activities, while teachers, accountants, and other staff can work efficiently from their phones, tablets, or laptops.

  • Administrator Manage school profile, classes, subjects, staff, students, attendance, results, and promotion.
  • Accountant Manage school fees, other income, payroll, expenditure, and financial reports.
  • Subject Teacher & Form Master Enter scores, comments, attendance, and other information needed to produce student report sheets.
  • Students & Parents Access student profiles, results, and academic progress online.
